# PBR material sources

Photo-based material maps downloaded from Poly Haven. License: CC0.

- [Kitchen Wood](https://polyhaven.com/a/kitchen_wood): timber grain, roughness and normal maps. Recolored for warm sauna timber / exterior decking.
- [Rock Boulder Dry](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry): stone coloration, roughness and normal maps. Recolored for charcoal basalt and pale paving.
- [Rough Linen](https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_linen): fiber structure, roughness and normal maps. Recolored for unbleached linen.
- [Wood Table](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table): downloaded as a candidate; not used in the final scene.

All maps are 2K JPGs. Original download URLs and MD5 hashes are recorded in `manifest.json`. Active texture images are packed into the final `.blend` file.
